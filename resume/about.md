## Who I Am

Pioneer, builder, and writer. Exploring minds and meaning.

For 25 years I've followed ideas to the edge of what's new and built the first version. Low-code before it had a name. AI strategy before it was on every roadmap.

Now: Growth Lab, where I help people multiply what they can do with frontier AI, and essays on minds and meaning.

What connects the work isn't a field. It's the habit of asking what's actually new. I'm self-taught: software architecture, economics, epistemology, philosophy. I study whatever the territory demands. I design systems, write frameworks, and search for good explanations.

My essays live at [buddy-williams.com/writings](https://buddy-williams.com/writings). Good places to begin:

- **More Better: Modeling and the Frame Problem:** every thought is a model, and most failures come from what the model leaves out
- **Computer People:** why LLMs can create knowledge, and why AGI constitutes personhood
- **Economics in the Intelligence Age:** a layered model of economics, from scarcity to AI
- **The Moving Line:** who gets repriced, who evaporates, and who captures what opens next as AI moves across work
- **Technohumanism:** the arc of humanity, from scarcity to abundance to biological misalignment
- **Cyclic Rationality:** recovering imagination before verification

## Intellectual Roots

Critical rationalism. Systems thinking. Economics as coordination under uncertainty.

**Thinkers who shaped me:** Karl Popper, David Deutsch, F.A. Hayek, Thomas Sowell, Carl Sagan.

I'm drawn to thinkers who revealed limits, like Gödel, Turing, and Haidt, and to the question of how we build systems that remain humane as intelligence becomes abundant.

## Core Question

> How can humans design systems (technical, economic, cultural) that remain humane, adaptive, and truth-seeking as intelligence becomes abundant?
